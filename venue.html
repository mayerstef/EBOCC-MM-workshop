Public transport

Use the STIB app to plan metro, tram, bus and train connections, with real-time information. Train tickets can be purchased through the SNCB app or at the station.

Enter Schuman as your destination. The venue is approximately a 10-minute walk from the station. Schuman has a train station, metro lines 1 and 5, and several bus connections.

Brussels AirportDirect trains to Brussels-Schuman take approximately 15–20 minutes; some journeys require a change. Alternatively, take Airport Line bus 12. A taxi takes around 20–30 minutes, depending on traffic.

Allow extra time: works around the Schuman roundabout have affected traffic and bus stops. Check your journey planner for the latest routes and service updates.

Building access

Registration deadline: 16 November 2026. Advance registration is required to enter the meeting building. Complete the building-access Excel form provided by the organisers and return it to the workshop team by this date.

Bring the same ID used for registration and arrive by 09:00 to allow time for the entrance procedure.

If you have not received the access form, please contact the workshop team.

Programme and preparation

The day combines short presentations by the EBOCC team with group discussions. No formal presentation is expected from participants.

The agenda will be shared with participants. A few days before the workshop, we will also send a link to a shared folder with background information to help prepare the discussions.

Catering

Coffee, refreshments and lunch will be provided. A registration form will be sent shortly so you can indicate any dietary requirements or allergies.

Travel and accommodation

VLIZ will reimburse travel and accommodation costs for in-person participants under the following conditions.

  1. Get approval before booking travel. Send your preferred flight or train option to the workshop team so we can check that it complies with the travel guidelines.
  2. Book your own travel. Choose economy-class flights or train tickets and the most economical suitable option.
  3. Book your own hotel. Up to two nights are covered, at a maximum of EUR 220 per night, including breakfast.
  4. Keep your supporting documents. For travel, retain tickets or boarding passes and proof of payment. For accommodation, retain the hotel invoice showing your name, stay dates and amount paid.
  5. Submit your reimbursement claim. Read the rules in the Excel reimbursement template before completing it. Combine the completed form and your tickets and receipts into a single PDF, and send it as soon as possible after the workshop to accounting@vliz.be and marina.diaz@vliz.be.
  6. Payment. Once your claim has been checked and approved, VLIZ accounting will reimburse the bank account specified on your form. We will keep you informed of the expected timing.

If you need the reimbursement template, please contact the workshop team.

Additional conditions

  • Meals: coffee and lunch during the workshop are provided and should not be included in your claim.
  • Cancellation: let us know as soon as possible if you can no longer attend. We can free your place and, if appropriate, discuss whether a colleague can attend instead.
  • Combined trips: if your trip includes unrelated business before or after the workshop, indicate the dates and times of your departure and return.
